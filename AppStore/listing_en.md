# App Store listing (English)

**Name:** PDF Compressor Ultra

**Subtitle:** Shrink PDFs, keep text sharp

**Promotional text:**
Make PDFs dramatically smaller: photos are re-encoded with modern JPEGli, while text and vector graphics stay crisp and searchable. 1-bit JBIG2 mode for scans.

**Description:**
PDF Compressor Ultra reduces the size of PDF files without turning pages into pictures. Text stays text — you can still select, copy and search it, and fonts and vector graphics keep their full quality.

WHAT GETS COMPRESSED
• Photos and scans are downsampled to the resolution you choose, based on their actual size on the page, and re-encoded with JPEGli — 25–35% smaller than regular JPEG at the same quality.
• Text, fonts and vector graphics are compressed losslessly; duplicate fonts and images are merged and unused data is removed.

5 PRESETS AND MANUAL MODE
• Strong — 72 dpi, quality 50%
• Optimal strong — 100 dpi, 60%
• Optimal — 120 dpi, 75%
• Medium — 144 dpi, 75%
• Light — 300 dpi, 80%
• Manual resolution and quality

COLOR, GRAYSCALE AND BLACK & WHITE
• Grayscale for the whole document.
• 1-bit black & white with JBIG2 and CCITT G4 — scanned contracts and documents become 5–20× smaller with sharp text. JBIG2 is lossless, with no symbol substitution.

MASTER MODE
Pick pages or individual images that must stay untouched, and set the color mode per page.

MORE
• Batch processing: drop many files or a whole folder, sort by name or size.
• Per-image report: format, resolution and size before and after.
• Password-protected PDFs (RC4, AES-128, AES-256).
• Repairs damaged files.
• Everything runs on your Mac, offline. Your files never leave your computer.
• Optimized for Apple Silicon.
